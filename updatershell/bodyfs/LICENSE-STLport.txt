The gen-1 updater-body templates in this directory — CXD4105.fdat,
CXD4115.fdat, CXD4115_ilc.fdat and CXD4120.fdat — statically link STLport into
`libupdaterbody` (see `updatershell/platform/vars.mk`, `LIBS_UPDATER_GEN1`). The
gen-2 (CXD4132) and gen-3 (CXD90014) bodies use the camera's own standard
library and do not contain STLport.
